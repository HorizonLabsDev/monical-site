Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. MoniCal works without signing in. Your data stays on your device and in your iCloud.

What happens after the 7-day trial?

The subscription starts at the price shown. If you cancel in iOS Settings at least 24 hours before the trial ends, you pay nothing.

Is there a one-time purchase?

Yes. Pro Lifetime costs $34.99 once and unlocks all Pro features permanently.

Can I use MoniCal for free?

Yes. The calendar, recurring entries, categories, 3 reminders and 1 budget are free.
